Built from field experience

現場経験を、使いやすい道具へ

Matsumoto Boundary のアプリは、日本の建設現場で監督業務と現場作業の両方を経験してきた開発者が、実務で使いやすいことを重視して開発しています。

The developer has worked on Japanese construction sites in both site management and hands-on field work. The tools are designed from practical experience to be simple, fast and useful on site. We hope users around the world can experience a small part of Japanese construction craftsmanship — “TAKUMI”.

日本の建設関連国家資格

  • 1級建築施工管理技士
  • 2級建築士
  • 2級土木施工管理技士

Japanese national construction-related qualifications.
